The two problems

Parser. The shared parser reads every date month first, and these tenants write the day first. A date comes out with day and month swapped. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

Hint. The booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first, then reads the date day first. Only reports through that form belong here. The same tenants’ app and email reports go through the parser. Won’t fix: booking-v3 form retired.

How to tell them apart

Read the tenant and the channel, not the subject or the intake tag. Both problems produce the same subjects, because the subject describes what the reporter saw. The intake tag is a guess from the browser, as the “tagged as” column shows. causes.ts holds the rule, and the triage page lets you try it. Its subject-matching rule is how these two problems ended up in one ticket.
